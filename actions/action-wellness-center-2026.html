FundingAppropriated

September 17, 2026

Funded the CMSE Graduate Student Wellness Center

The GSO increased Wellness Center funding from $500 to $1,500 for the year, 300% of last year's budget.

Funding decision

$1,500 for the year

This funding sustains the center's current work, expands available supplies, and creates room to test new ways of supporting CMSE graduate students.

$1,500Total Wellness Center budget

Allocation

How the funding will be used

$500

Continue current work

Funding will support the supplies and services the Wellness Center already provides to CMSE graduate students throughout the year.

$500

New supplies section

Funding will establish a new supplies section, broadening the resources available to students through the Wellness Center.

$500

Experimental ideas budget

Funding will be reserved to try new Wellness Center ideas and learn which additional offerings best serve CMSE graduate students.

Access and feedback

Visit and shape the Wellness Center

Visit the Wellness Center in the first-floor graduate student space. Use this QR code to suggest supplies or other additions you would like the center to offer.
