Created and Launched the New CMSE GSO Website

The GSO created this website to bring information that was previously spread across many places onto one student-focused platform.

Outcome

One place for CMSE graduate-student information

The website makes practical resources, department information, and GSO work easier to find and navigate from one place.

What it includes

Resources, events, and public progress

The site brings together guides for graduate life, department resources, GSO events and announcements, and a public record of GSO actions and progress. Its clear structure also makes it possible to share the site's URL with a language model and ask it to search, understand, and help navigate the available information.
