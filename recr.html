Responsible & Ethical Conduct of Research

Responsible and Ethical Conduct of Research (RECR) training is required for all graduate students at Michigan State University. CMSE PhD students follow the standard Graduate School requirements for doctoral students; CMSE does not currently add additional departmental RECR requirements.

RECR requirements are tracked throughout your degree. Doctoral students must complete the initial requirements before passing the comprehensive examination and entering candidacy, and must continue completing annual refresher training afterward.

01 / Start here

Year 1

Complete the four required MSU Graduate School RECR CITI modules during your first academic year.

  • Introduction to the Responsible Conduct of Research
  • Authorship
  • Plagiarism
  • Research Misconduct

The College of Engineering enrolls first-year doctoral students in a D2L course intended to help students navigate and complete their first-year RECR requirements. It helps students complete the standard requirements; it is not an additional CMSE requirement.

Start CITI training ->

Log in through Michigan State University using your MSU credentials and complete the MSU Graduate School RECR Program.

02 / Add breadth

Year 2

During your second academic year, complete 3 of the 6 available supplemental CITI modules.

  • Collaborative Research
  • Conflicts of Interest
  • Data Management
  • Mentoring
  • Peer Review
  • Financial Responsibility

You only need to complete three of these six for the Year 2 requirement. The supplemental modules may not appear in CITI until the four Year 1 modules have been completed.

03 / Before candidacy

6 Hours of Discussion-Based Training

Doctoral students must complete at least 6 hours of discussion-based, instructor-led RECR education before completing the comprehensive examination and entering candidacy.

Possible ways to earn these hours include:

  • Graduate School RECR workshops
  • Department or college workshops
  • Brown-bag discussions
  • Approved academic coursework covering RECR topics
  • Approved group discussions led by faculty or another facilitator
Graduate School RECR Workshops ->

For students entering graduate school in Fall 2022 or later, one-on-one advisor discussions do not satisfy the initial six-hour discussion-based requirement. If you are unsure whether a course, workshop, or event counts, ask the Graduate School.

04 / Keep current

Year 3 and Beyond

Beginning in Year 3, CMSE PhD students must complete 3 hours of RECR refresher training every academic year until graduation.

Possible refresher activities include unused supplemental CITI modules, Graduate School RECR workshops not previously used, department or college RECR activities, RECR-related courses or workshops, and one-on-one or group discussions with an advisor.

Important distinction: Advisor discussions may count toward Year 3+ refresher training, but should not be confused with the original 6-hour discussion-based requirement. Refresher training cannot normally be completed early for future years.

05 / Confirm your record

Check Your RECR Progress

Your official RECR record is available through MSU's Student Information System.

View RECR progress in SIS ->

After logging in, open Academic Progress and select RECR. CITI modules and many Graduate School workshops are automatically transferred into the university tracking system, but newly completed training can take time to appear.

Advisor Discussions

For Year 3+ refresher training, a doctoral student may submit eligible advisor discussions through SIS: Academic Progress → RECR Discussion Form. The advisor must verify the discussion before it counts.

This is not a method for satisfying the original 6 discussion hours.

Keep in mind

A few things worth knowing

  • CMSE follows the Graduate School doctoral RECR requirements and does not add extra departmental requirements.
  • Complete Year 1 modules during Year 1 and 3 supplemental modules during Year 2.
  • Complete the 6 discussion hours before your comprehensive exam/candidacy.
  • Beginning in Year 3, complete 3 refresher hours every year until graduation.
  • RECR completion must appear in SIS to count officially.
  • Missing annual RECR requirements can result in a hold on your student record.
  • Funding agencies or individual research projects may require additional training.
