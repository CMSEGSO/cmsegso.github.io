05 · A shared resource

Graduate Wellness Center

The CMSE GSO maintains a wellness table on the first floor of the graduate space to give students easy access to basic personal care items and other wellness resources during busy graduate-school days.

At the table

What You May Find

Items can include:

  • Toothpaste
  • Mouthwash
  • Deodorant
  • Shampoo
  • Body wash
  • Razors
  • Feminine hygiene products
  • Other basic personal-care items
  • Information about campus resources, events, and fitness opportunities

Supplies vary depending on what is currently available.

Keep it going

How It Works

Take what you need.
Leave what you can.

Students can stop by whenever they need something and may also contribute appropriate items if they would like to help keep the table stocked.
