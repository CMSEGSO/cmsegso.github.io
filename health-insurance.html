01 / Use the plan

How your GA health insurance works

You have an Aetna health insurance plan. MSU pays your premium, but what you pay for care depends on the service and where you receive it.

  1. You need medical care.Decide whether you need routine, urgent, or emergency care.
  2. Choose where to go.Start at Olin or go directly to an Aetna in-network provider.
  3. No specialist referral is required.Some services can still require Aetna precertification.
  4. Show your Aetna insurance card.Confirm network status before non-emergency care.
  5. The provider bills Aetna.Aetna processes the claim using the GA plan's rules.
  6. Aetna pays its share.The amount depends on the service and whether the provider is in network.
  7. You pay your share.Any applicable deductible, copay, coinsurance, or non-covered charge is your responsibility.

Your choice of care

Olin is not your only option

You can use Olin Health Center, choose another primary care physician (PCP) in Aetna's nationwide provider network, or go directly to an in-network specialist. If you go outside Olin, confirm directly with the provider that they are in network with Aetna.

Example Need a dermatologist? Find an Aetna in-network dermatologist → make an appointment → show your Aetna card. No Olin referral is required.

Referral and precertification are different. You do not need a referral for specialty care, but Aetna may require advance approval for certain procedures, imaging, admissions, or medications. Check the plan document or contact Aetna before scheduled care.

03 / Decode the language

What those insurance words mean

Premium
The price just to have insurance. MSU pays the eligible GA's premium, so the GA pays $0.
Copay
A fixed amount you pay for certain types of care or prescriptions.
Deductible
An amount you may pay toward certain services before the plan begins paying its share.
Coinsurance
A percentage split: you pay part of the allowed cost and Aetna pays the rest.
Out-of-pocket maximum
The most you pay during the plan year for covered services that count toward the limit. Exclusions and plan rules still apply.
In network
A provider with contracted Aetna rates. This generally keeps your costs lower.
Out of network
A provider without that contract. Costs can be substantially higher and additional charges can apply.
Precertification
Aetna's advance approval for certain services. It is not the same as a referral.

04 / More than office visits

Prescriptions, mental health, and therapy

Prescription coverage

CVS Caremark administers the pharmacy benefit, with a nationwide participating pharmacy network and more than 25 medications available at no cost.

CVS Caremark is the pharmacy benefit manager. You do not have to fill every prescription at a CVS retail store; use a participating pharmacy in its network.

Mental and behavioral health

The GA plan includes mental health and substance-use treatment, including outpatient office visits, telemedicine consultations, and inpatient care subject to the plan's cost sharing and rules.

Physical, occupational, and speech therapy

The current plan lists unlimited visits for covered outpatient physical, occupational, speech, and cognitive therapies when medically necessary. Cost sharing depends on where care is received.

Before scheduled services

Confirm that both the provider and facility are in network. Ask whether the service needs precertification and request an estimate of your cost.

05 / Graduate Assistants

Automatic enrollment and coverage dates

Eligible Graduate Assistants are automatically enrolled in MSU's Graduate Assistant Health Plan through Aetna. MSU pays the entire GA premium. For the 2026-2027 plan year, the annual cost of an individual GA policy is $4,188, and the GA pays $0 toward that premium.

Academic HealthPlans (AHP) handles enrollment, waivers, billing, and portal access. An automatically enrolled GA should wait for the AHP/Care26 email rather than creating a voluntary student account.

06 / Family coverage and changes

Dependents and qualifying life events

Graduate Assistants may voluntarily enroll eligible dependents. Dependents generally must be enrolled for the same coverage period as the GA who holds the policy.

Qualifying life events

Certain changes may allow you to enroll or change dependent coverage outside normal enrollment.

  • Loss of other health coverage
  • Marriage or divorce
  • Birth or adoption of a child
  • Entry into the United States by an eligible dependent

Act quickly: The GA plan generally requires notice and an election within 30 days of the qualifying event.

07 / Coverage by status

Graduate students without a GA appointment

Students who are not automatically enrolled may be able to voluntarily purchase the MSU Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP). Students enrolled in at least one credit, including certain fellowship students, may be eligible.

Completely voluntary students may also have access to three-month quarterly periods costing $997 each. Review eligibility and current enrollment instructions on MSU's Voluntary Student Health page.

International students

International students are automatically enrolled in MSU's Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP) under separate MSU requirements. Use MSU HR's Auto-Enrolled International Student Health information rather than assuming GA rules apply.

If you are both an international student and a GA, verify your AHP enrollment status rather than buying duplicate coverage. MSU states that the student-plan record updates to the GA plan after the assistantship is processed.

08 / Separate elections

Dental, vision, and health-plan waivers

Dental insurance

Adult dental insurance is separate from the main medical plan. Students and GAs may voluntarily purchase MSU's student and GA dental plan; MSU pays 50% of the premium for eligible GAs.

Vision insurance

Vision insurance is also a separate voluntary benefit. Check current enrollment periods, coverage, and pricing.

Waiving medical coverage

Some automatically enrolled students may waive the MSU plan if they have qualifying alternative coverage. A separate waiver is generally required each semester.

Review official waiver requirements ->

Do not assume cancellation

Having another policy does not automatically cancel MSU coverage. Complete the official waiver process by the applicable deadline when required.
