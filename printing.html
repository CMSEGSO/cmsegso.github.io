03 / If the printer beeps

Troubleshooting

If you hear three or four loud beeps immediately after printing, the printer may be trying to draw paper from a location that is empty.

  1. Log in on the printer itself.
  2. Change the paper location by clicking the box in the bottom-left corner, then choose Letter.
  3. Alternatively, move paper to the smaller tray on the right side.
